# Trenchcoons
Two broke raccoons in pursuit of a driving liscence end up on their most trecherous test yet: stranded on an island with SCARY FOXES. Coordinate your driving skills and ESCAPE ALIVE!!!  

Player 1: Controls Steering with A & D

Player 2: Controls Accelerator and Brake with UP and DOWN arrow keys.
